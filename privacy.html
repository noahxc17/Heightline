Privacy notice

The short version: your Heightline data stays on your device. We don't have accounts, and we can't see what you enter.

What stays on your device

Your measurements, daily routine and door photos are saved in your browser on this device. They aren't uploaded to us. Heights you type into the calculator aren't saved at all, unless you save them in the Growth tracker.

When you add a door photo, Heightline makes a smaller copy, which removes location data and other hidden photo details. Only that copy is saved.

Backups and sharing

A backup file contains your measurements, routine and photos, and it isn't locked with a password. Keep it private. Anything you choose to copy, save or share, like a Growth Wrapped card, is up to you.

Your choices

You can remove single measurements or photos, or use Delete my data on the Track tab to remove everything from this device. Clearing your browser data can remove it too, so download a backup first if you want to keep it.

Website hosting

Like most websites, our hosting provider may process technical details such as your IP address and device type to deliver the site and keep it secure. Heightline doesn't use advertising or analytics.

Children

Heightline may be used by young people. If you're under 18, it's a good idea to use it with a parent or carer, and to ask them before sharing anything from the app.

Heightline's estimates

Height estimates are rough and haven't been tested for accuracy. They aren't medical advice.
